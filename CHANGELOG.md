## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-29

### Added

- Provenance-aware compiler with separate inherited and overlay inputs.
- Untrusted and compatible profiles.
- Exact inherited secret delegation and explicit inherited-control escape hatch.
- Versioned, evidence-backed runtime-control corpus and JSON subpath export.
- Windows case-insensitive key identity and null-prototype output.
- Value-free receipts, structural validation, and resource budgets.
- ESM and CommonJS builds with TypeScript declarations.
